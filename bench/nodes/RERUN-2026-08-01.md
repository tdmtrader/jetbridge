## What this says about the platform

The two fixes that mattered were not prompt work. Restoring a working CLI and
a working output-builder handshake is what let both nodes complete with their
budgets enforced; the prompt patterns from the first pass (write the record
early, defer the output mechanism to the platform, state the contract
vocabulary) carried over unchanged and kept working.

The one durable agent-quality gap is unchanged and is not a platform problem:
the reviewer finds the right code and reasons about the wrong property, and it
does not follow a value out of the diff to its writer. That is the capability
question a code-intelligence sidecar or an `impacted-surface` node would
address, and it remains the most valuable open experiment.
